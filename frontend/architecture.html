Data Flow

1 Upload

User uploads TXT, image, or PDF.

2 OCR / Text Reader

Python reads text from the document.

3 Extractor

The app extracts invoice fields.

4 Validator

The app checks risk rules.

5 SQLite

The invoice is saved in the database.

6 Dashboard

Charts and tables show business results.

7 BI Reports

The app exports CSV and JSON reports.

Project Structure

app.py

Starts the Flask app and registers routes.

routes/

Controls dashboard, upload, OCR, reports, and downloads.

services/

Stores business logic like OCR, extraction, validation, and export.

data/

Stores the SQLite database file.

frontend/

Stores HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the visual dashboard.

uploads/

Stores uploaded documents and OCR text files by year and month.

reports/

Stores exported CSV and JSON business reports.

Interview Explanation

I built a document automation system with Python, Flask, SQLite, OCR, Pandas-style reporting logic, and a JavaScript dashboard. The system receives invoice documents, extracts business data, validates risk, stores results in SQLite, and generates dashboard insights and BI reports.
